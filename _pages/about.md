Hi there! I'm a second-year PhD student in the Linguistics Program at the University of South Carolina, advised by [Lewis Esposito](https://sites.google.com/view/lewisesposito/home). My research interests center on language and gender. I'm broadly interested in how gender is expressed, perceived, and processed through language. I enjoy exploring these questions from different perspectives and drawing on whatever methods best suit the question at hand. My current work brings together approaches from sociophonetics, sociolinguistics and psycholinguistics, and I'm always excited to expand my methodological toolkit.

I received my master's degree in Language Studies, in the Corpus Linguistics stream, from City University of Hong Kong (CityU), where I was supervised by [Aini Li](https://ainili-linguist.github.io/) and [Jackie Yan-Ki La](). My master's thesis examined dependency relations between adjectives and gendered nouns in Chinese corpora. After graduating, I worked as a research assistant at City University of Hong Kong and later at the Centre for Research on Chinese Language and Education (CRCLE) at The Education University of Hong Kong.
